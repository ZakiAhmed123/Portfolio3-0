Unifying Scheduling to Drive Efficiency and Scale: Modernizing Net Health's Core Workflow for Business Growth

Unifying Patient Scheduling for Efficiency, Consistency and Scalability

Transforming therapy management through unified scheduling and improved information architecture

Project Overview

Role

Principal Product Designer

Company

NetHealth (Neudesic/IBM)

Timeline

2024

Platform

Web & Tablet

NetHealth provides therapy management software for home health and hospice agencies. The platform helps clinicians schedule patient visits, document care, and manage therapy programs efficiently.

The Challenge

NetHealth's scheduling system had evolved into multiple separate schedulers across different areas of the platform. This fragmentation created several critical issues:

Inconsistent User Experience: Each scheduler had different interactions, workflows, and visual designs, creating confusion and requiring clinicians to learn multiple systems.

Development Inefficiency: Maintaining multiple codebases meant bug fixes and feature updates had to be implemented separately across each scheduler.

Reduced Transparency: Critical information about patient visits and clinician schedules was buried or missing, making it difficult to coordinate care effectively.

The Solution

I led the design effort to consolidate all scheduling functionality into a single, unified scheduler with a tablet-first approach. The new design focused on three key pillars:

1

Unified Experience

Consolidated all scheduling workflows into one consistent interface with standardized interactions and patterns.

2

Enhanced Transparency

Improved information architecture to surface critical details at the right time, reducing cognitive load for clinicians.

3

Tablet-First Design

Optimized the interface for tablet devices, the primary tool used by clinicians in the field during patient visits.
